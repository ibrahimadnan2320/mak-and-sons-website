Payment stages

Both quotation documents include staged payment schedules that total 100%. The stage percentages are tied to construction milestones; ask for the latest signed schedule before making a payment.

Grey structure starts with a 3% advance, followed by staged payments for excavation, lean/PCC, DPC, brickwork, slabs, plaster, raw floors, exterior work and completion.

Finishing has its own milestone schedule, beginning with a 15% advance in the finishing quotation.
