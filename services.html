Construction, finishing and homes

Practical services for the home you have in mind.

MAK & SONS works across Lahore on grey structure construction and home finishing, and also builds and sells completed homes. Your written quotation defines the agreed scope.

01 / Grey structure

The essential structure of a home.

The quotation covers foundation excavation and brickwork, masonry, lintel and slab work, wall plaster, concealed electrical piping, sanitary piping, raw floor, roof parapets and waterproofing, door frames, stairs and listed slabs and wardrobe bases.

The grey-structure quotation names first-class brick; Mughal or Madinah 60-grade steel; Bestway, Lucky or Flying cement; Sargodha crush; local sand; and Popular or Dura sanitary pipe.

02 / Finishing

The materials and details that make it feel like home.

Finishing scope may include floor and wall tiles, ceiling sealing, paint, kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, doors, aluminium work, stair stone and railing, selected lights, mirrors and sanitary fixtures. Quoted allowances and specifications apply.

The written quotation lists material brands and item allowances. Any selection above an allowance, front-elevation tiles, non-covered work, appliances and other listed exclusions are charged separately or remain the owner’s responsibility.
